1. About these terms

These terms apply when you ask Neat Narrative Co. for a quote or book a clean with us. They are written for consumers, but they also apply if you are a business booking domestic or commercial cleaning.

Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or any other law that protects you. If any part of these terms turns out to be unenforceable, the rest of them still stand.

2. Quotes

Quotes are free and there is no obligation to book. We quote per property rather than per hour, because two properties of the same size can need very different amounts of work.

A quote is based on the information you give us. If, when we arrive, the property turns out to be substantially different from what we were told — much larger, or in a very different condition — we will tell you before doing anything that changes the price, and you can decide what you would like us to do.

3. Booking a clean

A booking is made when we confirm a date and time with you and you accept the quote. From that point these terms apply to that job, and the details in your quote apply to the work itself.

4. Access to your property

We need to be able to get in and out safely, and to work without risk to ourselves or your property. Please tell us in advance about anything we should know: pets, alarm codes, parking restrictions, a locked gate, or a key held elsewhere.

You do not have to be at home. If you would rather not be, we will agree with you how access will work before the first visit, and we will never pass on details of how to get into your property to anyone who does not need them.

5. What we ask of you

  • Water and power. We need a working supply of both. If either will be off, please tell us beforehand so we can rearrange.
  • Pets. Tell us what to expect. We are happy to work around animals, but we need to know they are there.
  • Fragile and valuable items. Please move anything irreplaceable, extremely fragile or unstable before we arrive. If that is not possible, point it out and we will leave it alone.
  • Hazards. Tell us about anything that could be dangerous — asbestos, exposed wiring, broken glass, sharps, or a damaged surface we might make worse by cleaning.

6. Changing or cancelling a visit

Life changes, and we would always rather know than wonder. Tell us as early as you can and we will move the visit where we have the room.

Where we have reserved a slot for you and you cancel at short notice, we may ask you to cover the reserved time. We will never do that quietly: if it applies to your booking it will say so in your quote, in advance. If you cancel during the fourteen-day period that consumer law gives you, that right is unaffected.

If we ever have to change or cancel a visit, we will tell you as soon as we can and offer you the next available slot.

7. Payment

Payment terms — how much, when, and by what method — are set out in your quote. We do not take payment through this website, and we will never ask you for card or bank details through it either.

8. If something is not right

Tell us as soon as you notice. If any part of a clean is not up to the standard we agreed, we will come back and put it right, and we will not charge you for doing so. The same applies if we damage something — see below.

We would much rather hear about a problem than not. A quick call is usually all it takes.

9. Liability and insurance

We are fully insured, and we are happy to confirm the details of that cover before we start work. We take responsibility for damage we cause through carelessness or through work that falls short of a reasonable standard.

There are a few things we cannot be responsible for, simply because they are outside our control: existing wear, damage or fault in a surface that cleaning did not cause, items that were already broken or unstable, or problems that arise because we were not told about something relevant — a mark that needed special care, or a hazard we could not have known about.

We are a cleaning company, so our liability is limited to the reasonable cost of putting right the cleaning or the damage concerned. Nothing here limits our liability where it cannot lawfully be limited.

10. Photographs and this website

We will not publish a photograph of your property, or any detail that identifies it, without your permission first. If you do give us permission, you can withdraw it at any time and we will take the image down.

The photographs in the gallery on this website illustrate the standard of finish we work to. They are not a record of other clients' properties.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy, call +44 7721 053157 or email hello@neatnarrative.co.uk. Tell us what happened and what you would like us to do. We will respond as quickly as we can and, wherever it is in our power, put it right.

12. Law and changes

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction over any dispute. We may update these terms from time to time; the version that applies to your booking is the one in force when you booked, and the date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
