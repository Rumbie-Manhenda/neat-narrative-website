About us

The story behind the name.

Neat Narrative Co. is a local cleaning company on the North East Essex coast. The name says something about how we work - care, order, and a job finished properly.

Why the name

Neat is the standard. Narrative is the reason we ask.

Every property has its own story. A family home that has just survived a renovation, a flat being handed back at the end of a tenancy, a holiday let waiting for its next guests - each one needs something slightly different.

So we do not turn up with one fixed routine and work through it regardless. We ask what the property is for, what matters most right now, and what would genuinely make a difference. Then we clean to that.

Every property has its own story. We just make sure yours is ready for its next chapter.

How we work

Four things we do not compromise on.

Detail-led

Skirting boards, switches, the tops of door frames and the corners others skip. The details are the job, not the extra.

Reliable

We arrive when we say we will. If something has to change, you hear it from us first rather than wondering where we are.

Insured

Fully insured, with cover in place before we set foot inside your property. We are happy to confirm the details before we start.

Eco-conscious

Products chosen to be kinder to homes, pets and the coastline we live on - and adjusted where someone has a sensitivity.

Local, properly

Based here. Working here.

We are not a national franchise passing your postcode on to whoever happens to be nearest. We work on this stretch of coast - we know the roads, the parking, the summer rush, and the difference a sea breeze makes to a set of windows.

It also means you can actually reach us. One local number, one person who knows your property, and a team you get to know rather than a different face every visit.
