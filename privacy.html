1. Who we are

Neat Narrative Co. provides residential and commercial cleaning in Frinton-on-Sea and the surrounding North East Essex coast. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, we are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice.

  • Email: hello@neatnarrative.co.uk
  • Phone: 01255 724005

2. What we collect

Mostly, we only hold what you give us when you ask for a quote or book a clean. Through the enquiry form on this website we ask for:

  • your name;
  • a phone number and an email address, so we can reply;
  • your postcode or town, so we can confirm we cover your area;
  • the type of property and the service you are interested in;
  • how often you would like us to attend (optional);
  • anything else you choose to tell us (optional);
  • any photographs or documents you choose to attach (optional).

Please do not send us special category information — for example details about health, or anything about a criminal conviction — through this form. If it is relevant to a clean, tell us by phone or in person instead, and we will agree how to record it. We never ask for card or bank details through this website.

3. How we use it, and why

  • To read your enquiry and reply to it, including preparing a quote.
  • To arrange, carry out and invoice the cleaning work you ask for.
  • To keep a record of what was agreed, so we can be consistent.
  • To meet our accounting and tax obligations.

We do not use your details for marketing, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anybody. If we ever wanted to send you something promotional, we would ask you first and you could say no.

4. Our lawful basis

  • Steps before a contract. Where you ask us for a quote, we process your information because it is necessary to take those steps at your request.
  • Performance of a contract. Where you book a clean, we process your information to provide it.
  • Legitimate interests. We keep sensible business records and protect our own legal position. We have weighed this against your rights and interests, and we only keep what is reasonable.
  • Legal obligation. We keep financial records for the period tax law requires.
  • Consent. Where we rely on consent — for example if you agree to us using a photograph — you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only where we need to:

  • with the providers who host this website and carry our email, acting on our instructions;
  • with our accountant, for records we are required to keep;
  • with the cleaner attending your property, who needs your name, address and any access arrangements — and nothing more;
  • with a regulator, the police or a court, where we are legally required to disclose it.

If we ever need to send your information outside the UK, we will make sure an appropriate safeguard is in place and tell you about it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client and booking records: for as long as you are a client, and up to six years afterwards, as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Photographs sent for a quote: deleted once the quote has been prepared, or sooner if you ask.

7. Cookies and this website

This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. The fonts, icons and photographs you see are served from this website itself rather than from a third party, so browsing it does not report your visit anywhere else.

If we ever add something that does set a cookie, this notice will be updated before it goes live, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

One practical note: if our quote system is ever unreachable, this website may offer to hand your enquiry to your own email program instead. In that case nothing is sent by the website itself — the message is sent from your own account, by you.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • ask us to restrict what we do with it;
  • object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • ask for your information in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where consent is what we rely on.

To use any of these, email hello@neatnarrative.co.uk or call 01255 724005. We will answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we hand over any information, and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

9. How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first — most things are easier to fix than to escalate. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time — for example if we change how the enquiry form works or add a new service. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
