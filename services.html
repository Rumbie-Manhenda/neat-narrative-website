What we do

Cleaning services, shaped around your property.

Six core services built on the same idea: agree what matters, hold to a standard, and leave a property genuinely ready for what comes next.

01

Regular home cleaning

A regular visit is the backbone of a well-kept home. We agree the rooms and the rhythm - weekly, fortnightly or monthly - then keep to it, so a house never drifts back to square one between visits.

  • Kitchens and bathrooms: surfaces, fixtures, tiles and floors
  • Bedrooms and living areas dusted, vacuumed and tidied
  • Internal glass, mirrors and everyday touch points wiped down
  • Bins emptied, sinks and drains left fresh
  • Anything that needs your attention reported straight back to you
Quote this service

02

Deep cleaning

A deep clean goes after everything a weekly visit cannot reach: the inside of appliances, the tops of door frames, the grout, the corners behind the furniture. It resets a property rather than tidying it.

  • Inside the oven, hob, extractor and microwave
  • Inside the fridge and freezer
  • Limescale removed from tiles, glass, taps and shower screens
  • Skirting boards, door frames and radiators
  • Behind and beneath movable furniture
Quote this service

03

End of tenancy cleaning

Handing back a property is stressful enough. We work to a room-by-room checklist, so the inventory check becomes a formality rather than a negotiation over the deposit.

  • A full property clean, worked through room by room
  • Inside cupboards, wardrobes and drawers
  • Appliances included - oven, hob, extractor and fridge
  • Bathrooms descaled and sanitised
  • Booked for a move-out, a move-in, or both
Quote this service

04

One-off & spring cleans

Not every clean needs to be a commitment. A single visit is often all it takes - before guests arrive, after building work, or simply because the season has turned.

  • Preparation before guests, a party or a viewing
  • Dust removal after building or decorating work
  • A seasonal refresh of the whole property
  • Getting a property ready for sale or valuation
  • No ongoing commitment required
Quote this service

05

Commercial & office cleaning

Workplaces need a different rhythm. We clean around your opening hours, so desks, kitchens and washrooms are fresh before the first person walks in.

  • Offices, studios, clinics and co-working spaces
  • Washrooms, kitchens and communal areas
  • Desks, screens and shared touch points sanitised
  • Waste and recycling handled to your routine
  • Early-morning, evening or weekend schedules
Quote this service

06

Holiday lets & second homes

A coastal let lives or dies on its changeover. We work to your turnaround time, so the property is genuinely guest-ready between stays rather than just tidy.

  • Changeover cleans between guests
  • Pre-arrival cleans after a quiet period
  • Beds stripped and remade, rooms reset
  • Kitchens and washrooms restocked to your list
  • Anything that needs attention reported straight back to you

Questions

The things people ask first.

Which areas do you cover?

Frinton-on-Sea and the villages around it, along the Tendring coast ; Walton-on-the-Naze, Kirby-le-Soken, Kirby Cross, Great Holland, Holland-on-Sea, Clacton-on-Sea and inland from there. If you are not sure whether we reach you, call +44 7721 053157 and we will tell you straight.

Do you clean homes, offices, or both?

Both. We look after family homes, rental properties and holiday lets, as well as offices, studios, clinics and shops. The standard is the same either way.

Are you insured?

Yes - we are fully insured, and we are happy to confirm the details of that cover before we start work.

What products do you use?

We favour eco-conscious products that are kinder to homes, pets and the coastline, and we can adjust what we use if anyone in the property has sensitivities. Just mention it when you enquire.

How do I get a price?

Send the quote form or call the office. We will ask a few questions about the property and what you need, then come back with a tailored price. There is no charge for the quote and no obligation to book.

Can you clean an empty property before a move?

Yes - end of tenancy and pre-move cleans are core services for us, and they are usually the ones that matter most when a deposit is at stake.

Can you look after a holiday let between guests?

Yes. Changeovers are a big part of what we do on this coast: we work to your turnaround time so the property is ready for the next arrival.

Ready for your next chapter?

Tell us about your property and we will come back with a plan and a clear price. No obligation, no pressure.
