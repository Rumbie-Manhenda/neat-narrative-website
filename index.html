Who we are

A local cleaning team with an eye for the details others miss.

Neat Narrative Co. is a residential and commercial cleaning company working across Frinton-on-Sea and the villages around it. We look after family homes, rental properties, holiday lets and workplaces - each one treated as if it were our own.

No rotating strangers, no rushed jobs and no corners cut. Just a small, consistent team who arrive when they say they will and leave a property genuinely ready for whatever comes next.
